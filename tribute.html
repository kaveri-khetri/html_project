About Chatrapati shivaji maharaj

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj (1630-1680) was a visionary Indian warrior king, the founder of the Maratha Empire, renowned for his military genius (especially guerrilla warfare), strong navy, and progressive administration, establishing Swarajya (self-rule) through courage, justice, and religious tolerance, becoming a revered national hero and inspiring symbol of Indian resistance and patriotism.

Achievements

  • Founded Maratha Empire
  • Military Genius
  • Developed a Navy
  • Fortification
  • Strategic Victories
  • Crowned Chhatrapati
